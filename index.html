Why solar, and why tracking?

Solar power is one of India's main clean energy sources. Installed solar capacity crossed about 168 GW by August 2026 (MNRE data reported in the press; check the official figure before citing). Most of it is still in fixed panels.

The sun moves

The sun moves about 15° every hour. A fixed panel is only square to the sun for a short time each day.

Angle matters

Output falls with cos θ, where θ is the angle between the sun and the panel. At 60° off-angle, a flat panel gets about half its maximum power.

Dust in Delhi: why cleaning matters

Dust settling on a panel blocks light and cuts output. The amount depends on local PM2.5 and on how long the panel goes without cleaning. Our project measures this loss against PM2.5 and studies when cleaning pays off.

  • Check the local air quality on the CPCB or official AQI app before a cleaning decision.
  • Clean panels only when the lost energy is worth more than the water and labour cost.
  • Use minimal water. Water is scarce in Delhi.

Questions people ask

Is a rotating panel always better?

No. It gives more energy when the sun is well off the panel's flat position, but it has moving parts that need maintenance. Our test compares the gain with the cost.

Is this a real power plant?

No. It is a small prototype to test the method. Results apply to the test site and setup only.

Can I build one?

Yes. The basic parts are an Arduino, a servo motor, two light sensors (LDRs) and small panels. The estimated cost is around ₹800 to ₹1,200.
